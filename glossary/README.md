# Glossary
术语表

Two resources to cut through the noise:
两个帮你穿透噪音的资源：

- **[terms.md](terms.md)** - 60+ AI terms explained. What people say vs what it actually means. --- **[terms.md](terms.md)** - 解释 60+ 个 AI 术语。人们怎么说 vs 它实际上是什么意思。
- **[myths.md](myths.md)** - 20 common AI misconceptions busted with what's actually going on. --- **[myths.md](myths.md)** - 拆解 20 个常见的 AI 误解，说明实际情况到底是什么。

Each term entry follows the pattern:
每个术语条目都遵循以下结构：
- **What people say** - the common (often wrong) understanding --- **人们怎么说** - 常见的（而且往往是错误的）理解
- **What it actually means** - the real definition --- **它实际上是什么意思** - 真实定义
- **Why it's called that** - origin of the name (when interesting) --- **为什么叫这个名字** - 名称的由来（如果有趣的话）
